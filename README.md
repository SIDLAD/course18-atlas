# Course 18 Atlas

An unofficial, interactive map of the MIT math major (Course 18): plan a degree by field, trace the road to any subject, compare two degrees, and find free resources for each course.

Live site: https://sidlad.github.io/course18-atlas/

Prerequisites come from the MIT Catalog and degree rules from the MIT Math Department's pages (checked October 2026). Not affiliated with or endorsed by MIT; confirm real plans with the department.
